链图标来源 / Chain icon sources

链的图标和名称是各自所有者的商标,这里只用来标明是哪条链。
Chain logos and names are trademarks of their respective owners and are used here only to identify each chain.

1. merlin.webp 以外的 *.svg:取自 npm 包 @web3icons/core@4.0.58(dist/svgs/networks/background/ 与 dist/svgs/tokens/background/),
   https://github.com/0xa3k5/web3icons ,只去掉了 class 属性。
   All *.svg files: from @web3icons/core@4.0.58 (networks/background and tokens/background), class attribute removed.
